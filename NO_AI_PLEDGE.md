# NO-AI PLEDGE

<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/52/No_AI.svg" alt="no AI" width="100" height="100">

**Pledge Status**: Active

**Enforcement Date:** September 19, 2026

**Maintained by:** Randall "R2" Dunkin

## 1. Declaration of Intent

I declare a strict commitment to avoid the intentional use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies, specified under "Terms of Compliance", throughout the entire project as of the Enforcement Date.

## 2. Terms of Compliance

### 2.a. Zero Intentional AI Generaton

No code, scripts, or logic have been generated, autocompleted, or suggested by AI tools, Large Language Models (LLMs), or neural-network-based copilots.

All text, documentation, commit messages, comments, and project materials are authored directly by me, a human, without AI drafting or editing assistance.

All images, graphics, audio, or other media assets included in this project are created manually or sourced from human-created materials.

### 2.b. Standard Exclusions & Clarifications

The use of traditional developer tools—including IDE syntax highlighters, type checkers, linters, basic spellcheckers, and compiler optimizations—does not violate this pledge.

## 3. Contribution

Any outside contribution accepted into this repository must adhere to this pledge.
